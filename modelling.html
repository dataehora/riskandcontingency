Run Monte Carlo simulation

Simulates the Total Cost (Direct Cost + Knock On) of every Regular Pooled Record risk with Status Open, weighted by its assessed Likelihood, across many trials to produce a cost distribution.

No open Regular Pooled Record risks yet — set a risk record's Record Type to Regular Pooled Record and its Status to Open in the Risk Register to include it in modelling.
