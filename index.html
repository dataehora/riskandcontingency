Risk & Contingency Management

A project risk register that lives on your machine, not in someone else's cloud

Record, assess and respond to project risks under a Risk Breakdown Structure, calculate EMV pre- and post‑mitigation, and report on exposure — all reading and writing directly to an Excel workbook in a folder you choose. No accounts, no server, no uploads.

⚠️
Folder connection isn't available in this browser.

This tool reads and writes your risk register directly on disk using the File System Access API, currently supported in Chrome, Edge and Opera. Open this page in one of those browsers to connect a folder.

Connect your risk register folder

Choose a folder on this device to store your project workbooks. The app will read the Excel risk register from it, and write every change straight back — the folder is your single source of truth.

Folder connected

Your risk register folder is connected. Head to Working Space to create or edit risk records, or Configuration to set up your Risk Breakdown Structure, impact areas and risk owners.

Create and manage risk records
